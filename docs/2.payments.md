The payment interface uses opaque IDs, integer amounts in minor units, and timestamps in epoch milliseconds. Store the provider IDs and your own `reference` together with the selected account. Never infer the structure of an ID.

## Hosted checkout and direct payments

For a hosted payment page, use the [README checkout example](../README.md#portable-checkout). `GetCheckoutSession` reads the current state after the payer returns. `session.payment` is set once Stripe creates the payment behind the session. An open session stays `pending` after a failed card attempt, because the payer can still retry.

Portable checkout disables [Stripe Managed Payments](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/managed-payments/set-up) on each session, even when the account turns it on by default. You stay the merchant of record, and you stay responsible for taxes. The portable contract does not supply the product tax codes or the other settings that Managed Payments needs. For that integration, call the Stripe SDK directly. The provider changes neither the account settings nor the Stripe-specific API.

`CreatePayment` charges saved methods, authorizes for a later capture, and confirms on the server. A synchronous card decline resolves with a `failed` payment that carries `failure`. A transport failure or an invalid request rejects. A `requires_action` payment carries a `redirectUrl` where the payer authenticates. Pass `returnUrl` for on-session payments, and send the payer to the redirect before you expect a terminal event. The portable API does not expose a Stripe client secret. For a Stripe.js integration, use the [Stripe-specific API](./5.legacy-stripe.md).

`authorizeOnly` leaves a payment `authorized`. `CapturePayment` captures once. It can capture less than the authorized amount, and Stripe then releases the rest. `CancelPayment` voids an uncaptured payment. `RefundPayment` makes full and partial refunds. A `pending` refund has not settled yet. To reconcile refunds, enable the [refund webhook events](./3.webhooks.md#event-subscriptions) and read `GetRefund`.

## Saved payment methods

Create a customer with `CreateCustomer` and persist its ID. To collect a mandate, set `customer` and `savePaymentMethod: true` on an on-session payment or checkout. The method appears in `ListPaymentMethods` after the payment succeeds or is authorized. `savePaymentMethod` needs a `customer`, and it cannot be combined with `offSession`.

An off-session `CreatePayment` needs a saved `paymentMethod`. When the bank asks for authentication, the payment declines, because the payer is not there to answer. `ListPaymentMethods` returns the saved methods newest first, card and other types alike. `DetachPaymentMethod` detaches one method. `DeleteCustomer` removes the customer and its saved methods, and keeps its past payments.

## Idempotency and uncertain outcomes

Before each logical mutation, generate one idempotency key and persist it. Retry the operation with the same key, the same parameters, and the same account. Stripe keeps the result of a POST idempotency key for at least 24 hours. After Stripe prunes it, the same key can create a new payment, refund, or subscription. This provider does not keep its own replay store.

After that window, reconcile the stored session, payment, refund, or subscription ID with your business ledger before you submit another mutation. If the first response was lost before you stored an ID, look up the Stripe records and your `reference` to decide whether another attempt is safe. Keep webhook deduplication separate from request idempotency.

Stripe does not apply idempotency keys to DELETE requests. A repeated customer deletion depends on how Stripe treats a deleted customer, not on a stored idempotency response. Immediate subscription cancellation writes a marker into the cancellation comment, as the [subscription guide](./4.subscriptions.md#cancellation-and-end-semantics) describes. That marker covers that one call and is not a general replay guarantee.

## Metadata and enrichment

The provider stores your `reference` under the reserved Stripe metadata key `antelope_reference`, and returns the rest of the metadata separately. Keep that key on the subscriptions the provider owns, and do not write it on other subscriptions.

`GetPayment` expands the latest charge to read the refunded total. For the same reason, payment webhooks retrieve `latest_charge` when the event holds only its ID. If that read fails, verification rejects, so Stripe retries the delivery instead of a refunded total of zero reaching your code. To find whether a charge belongs to a subscription, the provider also reads the invoice payments of the PaymentIntent. If that read fails, the call rejects instead of returning the payment without its subscription marker.
