# Take payments and retry safely

The payment interface uses opaque IDs, integer minor-unit amounts and epoch-millisecond timestamps. Store provider IDs and your own `reference` together with the selected account; never infer an ID's structure.

## Hosted checkout and direct payments

Use the [README checkout example](../README.md#portable-checkout) for a hosted payment page. `GetCheckoutSession` reads the current state after the payer returns, and `session.payment` becomes available once Stripe creates the underlying payment. An open session remains `pending` even after a failed card attempt because the payer can retry.

`CreatePayment` supports charging saved methods, manual authorization and server-side confirmation. A synchronous card decline resolves with a `failed` payment and `failure`; transport or invalid-request failures reject. A `requires_action` payment carries a `redirectUrl` for payer authentication. Use `returnUrl` for on-session payments and follow the redirect before expecting a terminal event. The portable API does not expose a Stripe client secret; use the [Stripe-specific API](./5.legacy-stripe.md) for an existing Stripe.js integration.

`authorizeOnly` leaves a payment `authorized`. `CapturePayment` captures once, optionally for less than the authorized amount and releases the remainder; `CancelPayment` voids an uncaptured payment. `RefundPayment` supports full and partial refunds; a pending result is not a settled refund. Enable the [refund webhook events](./3.webhooks.md#event-subscriptions) and read `GetRefund` for reconciliation.

## Saved payment methods

Create a customer with `CreateCustomer` and persist its ID. Set `customer` and `savePaymentMethod: true` on an on-session payment or checkout to collect a mandate; the method appears in `ListPaymentMethods` after payment succeeds or authorizes. Saving requires a customer and cannot combine with `offSession`.

An off-session `CreatePayment` needs a saved `paymentMethod`; it declines instead of prompting the absent payer for authentication. `ListPaymentMethods` returns saved methods newest first, including non-card methods. `DetachPaymentMethod` detaches a method, while `DeleteCustomer` removes the customer and its saved methods without deleting past payments.

## Idempotency and uncertain outcomes

Generate and persist one idempotency key per logical mutation before making the call. Retry the same operation with the same key, parameters and account. Stripe retains POST idempotency results for at least 24 hours; after pruning, the same key may create a new payment, refund or subscription. This provider does not add an indefinite replay store.

Beyond the retention window, reconcile the stored session, payment, refund or subscription ID and your business ledger before submitting another mutation. If the first response was lost before you stored an ID, inspect Stripe records and your reference before deciding whether another attempt is safe. Keep webhook deduplication separate from request idempotency.

Stripe does not apply POST idempotency semantics to DELETE operations. Customer deletion relies on Stripe's resource semantics, not a retained idempotency response. Immediate subscription cancellation uses a cancellation-comment marker as described in the [subscription guide](./4.subscriptions.md#cancellation-and-end-semantics); that marker is not a general replay guarantee.

## Metadata and enrichment

The provider stores your `reference` under reserved Stripe metadata key `antelope_reference`; it returns the remaining metadata separately. Preserve that key on owned subscriptions and avoid writing it on unrelated subscriptions.

`GetPayment` expands the latest charge to read refunded totals. Payment webhooks retrieve a string-valued `latest_charge` for the same reason. If charge enrichment fails, verification rejects: let Stripe retry rather than treating an unavailable refunded total as zero. Subscription-charge detection can also require an invoice-payment lookup; failures propagate rather than silently losing the subscription marker.
